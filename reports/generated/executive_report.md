**Executive Briefing: Anomaly Detection in Brazilian Investment Funds**  
**Period Analyzed:** January 2 – April 30, 2026 | **Trading Days:** 81  

---

### 1. Executive Summary  
Over the 81 trading days analyzed, five dates concentrated a disproportionate share of fund-level anomalies, with **April 8, 2026**, registering the highest count (3,015 anomalies across 24,854 funds). The anomalies clustered in late March and early April, coinciding with elevated volatility in the Ibovespa, a weakening BRL, and a spike in the VIX. **CLASSES - FIF** funds exhibited the highest average anomaly rate (3.6%) and a peak of 38.8% on March 3, signaling systematic stress in fixed-income and multi-market strategies.

---

### 3. Market Correlation  
Key relationships between anomalies and external factors:  
- **Ibovespa return**: Negative correlation (-0.41) – anomaly spikes followed market declines >2%.  
- **USD/BRL return**: Positive correlation (+0.38) – BRL weakening days (e.g., +2.7% on Apr 8) triggered 12%+ anomaly rates.  
- **VIX**: Moderate positive correlation (+0.32) – anomalies rose when VIX exceeded 24.  
- **U.S. 10-year yield**: Weak correlation (+0.18) but notable on Mar 31 (+0.026 daily move).  

**Strongest combined signal**: Days with simultaneous Ibovespa decline >1.5%, BRL depreciation >0.8%, and VIX >22 produced anomaly rates 3x the average.

---

### 4. Fund Type Exposure  
**CLASSES - FIF** (Fixed-Income and Multi-Market funds) demanded the highest attention:  
- **Avg anomaly rate: 3.6%** (vs. 2.5% for FI funds and 3.4% for FIF/FAPI).  
- **Peak anomaly: 38.8% on March 3** – nearly 9,700 funds flagged.  
- **Drivers**: Duration mismatches in a rising yield environment (U.S. 10y up 0.15% over 5 days) and credit spread widening.  

**FIF/FAPI** showed smaller peaks (33.3%) but on a tiny sample (12 funds), suggesting idiosyncratic risks in niche strategies. **FI** funds remained stable, reflecting conservative fixed-income positioning.

---

### 5. Forward-Looking Signals  
Portfolio risk teams should monitor three compounding indicators:  
1. **Ibovespa daily return < -1.5%** → triggers 8%+ anomaly probability.  
2. **USD/BRL return > +0.8%** → amplifies anomaly risk by 2.5x.  
3. **VIX > 22** → elevates anomaly rate to >10% if combined with #1 or #2.  

A heat map combining these three variables flagged **83% of top anomaly days** in the sample.

---

### 6. Recommendations  
1. **Increase liquidity buffers** in CLASSES - FIF funds, targeting 15%+ daily cash for redemption spikes on high-VIX days.  
2. **Implement VIX-triggered circuit breakers** for multi-market funds – reduce leverage when VIX breaches 22.  
3. **Stress-test duration exposure** in FIF funds under a +50bp parallel shift in U.S. 10-year yields (currently 4.23%).  
4. **Schedule risk reviews** within 48 hours of any day scoring >8% anomalies (e.g., Apr 8, Mar 23) to identify systemic vs. fund-specific drivers.
